# Stage 9M references (recalled from memory, NOT re-read in this stage)

Citations support the mechanistic claims behind the rule scope. PocketChem products are structural models, not validated predictions.

* Wacker-Tsuji: Tsuji, Synthesis 1984, 369; Clayden, Greeves, Warren, Organic Chemistry 2nd ed., ch. 40 (Pd(II) alkene oxidation, Markovnikov methyl ketone).
* Ring-closing metathesis: Grubbs, Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2006, 45, 3760; Clayden ch. 40.
* Mitsunobu: Mitsunobu, Synthesis 1981, 1; Swamy et al., Chem. Rev. 2009, 109, 2551 (SN2 inversion at secondary alcohols; pronucleophile pKa < ~13).
* Benzilic acid rearrangement: March's Advanced Organic Chemistry, rearrangements chapter (1,2-diketone + hydroxide, 1,2-aryl/alkyl shift; no enolizable alpha H).
* Pinacol coupling: Clayden ch. 23/24 (ketyl radical dimerization; SmI2, Mg/TMSCl); March.
* Ketonic decarboxylation: Renz, Eur. J. Org. Chem. 2005, 979.
* Dieckmann, acetoacetic and malonic ester syntheses: Clayden ch. 26; Vollhardt and Schore, Organic Chemistry, enolate chemistry chapters.
* Heteroarene EAS: Joule and Mills, Heterocyclic Chemistry 5th ed., chs. on furan, thiophene, pyrrole (alpha-selective; mild reagents: NBS, acetyl nitrate, Vilsmeier, SnCl4 acylation); Clayden ch. 43.
* Claisen-Schmidt: Clayden ch. 26 (crossed aldol with a non-enolizable aldehyde); Nielsen and Houlihan, Org. React. 1968, 16, 1.
